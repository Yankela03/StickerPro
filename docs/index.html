StickerPro Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yamkela Sotshozi (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use StickerPro.

Media processing

StickerPro converts images and videos into stickers on your device. Media selected by you is not uploaded to us, AppLovin, or Firebase. Temporary conversion files are deleted automatically after processing. Completed stickers remain in the app’s private storage until you remove the app or delete the stickers using available device or app controls. When you choose to add a sticker pack to WhatsApp, the app shares the sticker-pack files with WhatsApp at your request.

Online video links

If you submit a supported public video link, StickerPro connects to that source to retrieve the media you requested. The source may receive your internet address and standard connection information. Downloaded temporary video files are deleted after sticker processing. Only submit content you have permission to download and use.

Advertising

StickerPro uses AppLovin MAX to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AppLovin and advertising partners enabled in our MAX account may process information such as advertising or device identifiers, internet address, approximate location inferred from that address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and security. Consent or privacy choices are requested where required.

Learn more in AppLovin’s Privacy Policy and use AppLovin’s privacy choices where available.

Analytics and crash reporting

StickerPro uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose failures. Depending on configuration and consent, these services may process app interactions, device or other identifiers, device information, diagnostic information, and crash logs. StickerPro does not include the contents of your images, videos, stickers, filenames, or file paths in its own analytics events.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Permissions

StickerPro requests access to images or videos only so you can select media to edit. Network access supports requested downloads, advertising, analytics, and crash reporting. The Android advertising identifier may be used for advertising and measurement, subject to device settings and applicable consent.

Sharing

We do not sell your media. Information may be processed by AppLovin, advertising partners configured through MAX, Google Firebase, a video source you ask the app to access, and WhatsApp when you explicitly add or share stickers. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users, rights, and security.

Children

StickerPro is not directed to children, and the app must not initialize or use AppLovin services for a user who qualifies as a child under applicable law. Do not use the advertising-enabled version of StickerPro if you are a child under the applicable age in your country.

Security and retention

Media processing uses private app storage and encrypted network connections are used where supported. Temporary media is retained only as long as needed for conversion. Completed stickers remain locally until deleted or the app is removed. Advertising, analytics, and crash-report information is retained by the applicable providers according to their policies and account configuration.

Your choices

You can decline or change eligible advertising consent choices, reset or delete your Android advertising identifier in device settings, stop using online-link downloads, delete local stickers, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall StickerPro. Regional privacy rights may also be exercised through the providers linked above or by contacting us.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
