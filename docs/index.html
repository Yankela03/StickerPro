StickerPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how we handle information when you use the StickerPro Android app. We do not require you to create a StickerPro account.

Creating and exporting stickers

We use the photos and videos you select in Android’s picker or share with StickerPro to create static and animated stickers on your device. We do not upload your media or stickers to our advertising or analytics providers. Sticker packs are stored in the app’s private storage. When you choose to add a pack to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, StickerPro makes its sticker files and pack details available to that app. Any copies you export or share are handled by the receiving app.

Online media links

When you paste or share a supported public image or video link, StickerPro connects over HTTPS to the source and any media hosts it uses to retrieve the content you requested. Sources may include TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or a site hosting a media file directly. Those sites may receive the requested URL, your IP address, and standard connection information. They may also set temporary cookies used during retrieval. StickerPro does not ask for your login credentials for those services.

Status Saver

We use Android’s folder picker to request read access to the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business status folder you select. Android can remember this permission so you can return to Status Saver without choosing the folder again. We use it to list the status photos and videos in that folder, and copy items you choose to save to your device. StickerPro keeps a local record of saved items so it can mark them as saved.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob for banner ads, rewarded video ads associated with adding sticker packs to WhatsApp, and interstitial ads after a successful save in Status Saver. Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, advertising or other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, personalize ads where permitted, and prevent fraud and abuse.

We use Google’s consent tools to present applicable advertising privacy choices. When available, you can revisit them under Settings → Ad privacy choices. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how StickerPro is used and diagnose problems. Both are off by default. If you turn on Settings → Share usage and crash reports, Firebase may process usage events such as sticker creation and export, app and device information, app-instance or other identifiers, diagnostics, and crash reports. Our analytics events do not include your media, filenames, or file paths. You can turn collection off at any time. The app then resets local analytics data and requests deletion of unsent crash reports. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

Retention and deletion

  • We store working files and downloads in the app’s private cache and remove them during normal editing cleanup. If editing is interrupted, files may remain until you or Android clears the cache.
  • You can delete individual stickers or entire packs in My Stickers. Clearing StickerPro’s app storage or uninstalling it removes its private app data. Status copies saved to your gallery and packs you added to WhatsApp are managed separately in those apps.
  • Google retains data it receives for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting under its applicable policies and service settings. Clearing local app data does not delete data already sent to those services.

You can manage your Android advertising identifier in device settings. To ask about your privacy rights or request access to or deletion of personal data we hold, contact us using the email below.

Children

StickerPro is not directed to children.

We may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The date at the top shows when we last updated it.
